## 3. What the App does

CamGuard AI is a free inspection aid. It helps you look for possible indicators of hidden cameras in places such as hotel rooms, rentals, bathrooms and changing rooms. It offers a guided checklist with camera and flashlight, an infrared check, a Wi-Fi device list, a magnetic field scanner, an image analysis pipeline, and saved reports you can export as a PDF. Everything is processed on your device.

Some features depend on your phone's hardware, permissions and Android version. Some features, such as AI lens analysis, may be unavailable or limited in a given version of the App.

## 4. Important: no guarantee of detection

- The App cannot guarantee that a space is free of hidden cameras or other recording devices.
- Results are indicators, not proof. A flagged item may be harmless, and a clear result does not mean nothing is there. False alarms and missed devices are both possible.
- Many hidden cameras use no infrared light, do not appear on Wi-Fi and have no magnetic effect.
- The App is not a security, legal, forensic or professional surveillance-detection service. Do not rely on it as your only protection. Combine it with a careful manual inspection.
- If you believe you are in danger, contact local emergency services. If you find a device you believe is a hidden camera, do not tamper with it. Leave the area and report it to the host, staff or local authorities.
