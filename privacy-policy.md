# CamGuard AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Developer: AshNova Labs.

## Summary
CamGuard AI works on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, and no
advertising or analytics. Nothing you capture or scan is uploaded.

## What the app uses
- Camera: a live preview, and still images you choose to capture for Room Scan,
  AI Lens and Infrared checks. Images are processed in memory and discarded when
  you leave the screen. They are not saved or uploaded.
- Motion sensors: the magnetometer, for the Magnetic Field Scanner.
- Local network: the app listens for devices that announce themselves on your
  Wi-Fi. It does not probe, log in to, or connect to those devices.

## What is stored on your device
If you save a scan report, the app stores its date, duration, results, written
findings, recommendations and any notes you add, in a local database. It never
stores photos or video. App settings, such as your theme, are also stored
locally.

## What is never collected
Your name, email address, location, contacts, advertising ID, device identifiers
or usage analytics. There is no account to create.

## PDF export
Exporting creates a PDF on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose
to share or save it using the share sheet on your phone.

## Your controls
In Settings you can delete all scan reports or clear all local data. In Scan
History you can delete a single report. Uninstalling the app removes its data.
You can turn off any permission in your phone's system settings at any time.

## Limits of the app
CamGuard AI helps look for possible indicators of hidden cameras. It cannot
guarantee that a space is free of them. Always inspect manually as well. Do not
use the app to access devices you do not own.
